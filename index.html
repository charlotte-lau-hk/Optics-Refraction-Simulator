Snell's Law
n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂ = n₃ sinθ₃
In parallel layers, the angle of refraction at one boundary equals the angle of incidence at the next boundary (alternate angles). Thus intermediate parallel layers do not alter the final refraction angle.
Total Internal Reflection & Critical Angle
sin c = n₂ / n₁   (n₁ > n₂)
When light travels from an optically denser medium (larger n₁) to a less dense medium (smaller n₂) with incident angle θ > c, Total Internal Reflection (TIR) occurs.
